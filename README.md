## Evidence and publication boundaries / 証拠・公開範囲

The investigation covers 24 findings (F-001–F-024) and seven integrated conclusions (S-001–S-007) across immigration law, economic sectors, compliance, tourism, fees and JESTA. Government explanations are distinguished from measured effectiveness; projections are not observed effects. The editorial seven-gate review does not constitute independent external peer review.

本報告書は24件の認定事項と７件の統合結論を対象とする。行政の説明と測定された効果、将来推計と実績値を区別し、第三者による独立査読済みとは表示しない。
